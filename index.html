Data Architecture Reconciliation across Two Systems

Senior BI Developer
Microsoft Fabric Notebooks SQL Lakehouse
Challenge

The company had two parallel databases with different architectures and reporting logic, leading to inconsistent reports, manual reconciliation and delayed insights.

Action
  • Implemented a unified data architecture to reconcile the two systems.
  • Developed pipeline and ETL processes to ensure data consistency and accuracy.
  • Processes written in Fabric notebooks for increased collaboration, maintainability and documentation.
Result
  • Successfully reconciled data across both systems, ensuring consistent and accurate reporting.
  • One source of truth integrated in the Microsoft ecosystem.
  • Company can now have a reconciled view of their data for better decision-making.
